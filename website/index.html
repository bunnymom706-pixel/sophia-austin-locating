The catch

There isn't one. How is this free?

The property pays my fee when you sign. You pay nothing — not to search, not to tour, not to move in.

Same rent, same specials, same unit you'd get walking in off the street. The difference is you don't spend three weekends driving around Austin finding out which places are actually available and which ones will approve you.

What I do

Four kinds of hard search

Relocation

Moving to Austin sight unseen? I tour in person, send you video, and tell you what the listing photos left out — the highway noise, the walk to the mailroom, the parking.

Second chance

Eviction, broken lease, or a felony on your record? I know which communities will actually approve you, so you stop burning application fees just to get denied.

First-time renters

Never leased before? I'll walk you through income requirements, deposits, guarantors, and what you're signing. No question is too basic.

Luxury

High-rise, downtown, concierge, rooftop. I'll find the concessions that never get posted and the units that never hit the listing sites.
