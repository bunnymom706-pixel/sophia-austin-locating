FB MARKETPLACE AD SCRIPT v2 - Spirit Real Estate Group account
Paste and fill. One ad per property. Never publish from a script, always land as a draft and post it yourself.

WHAT CHANGED FROM THE ONE PLACE VERSION
- Signature line now reads Spirit Real Estate Group. Nothing else in the body identifies the brokerage.
- Fresh account, fresh wording. Do not reuse any slogan, header, intro, or CTA from the old ads. The lines below are new starting points, and you rotate them.
- Post from your personal profile, not a page. Warm the account up: 2 to 3 ads a day the first week, spaced hours apart, different photos and different phrasing on every one. Twelve near-identical ads in an hour is how the last account got flagged.
- Price in the body must match the Marketplace price field to the dollar.

=====================================================
RULES THAT DO NOT MOVE
- Full address goes in parentheses on line one for your reference only. Delete it before posting.
- No property name, no cross streets, no landmark that pins the building.
- No phone, no email, no link, no "text me," no "send you a list." Messenger is the only door.
- No fair housing language, no safety or crime language, no hashtags, no square footage, no finishes line.
- Never net effective rent. Base rent and the special are separate lines. Always say the special is on top of base rent, and name the minimum lease term it requires.
- Greystar and RPM Living communities: lead with TMLP (total monthly leasing price), base rent smaller underneath. No TMLP in hand, ask before writing.
- Three emoji max in the whole ad, none in the slogan, never the same one twice. 🐾 only on the pet line.
- Symbol dividers (✦ ✧ ✮ ⋆ ★ ❖ ⊹ ⁂ ∙) are not emoji and do not count. Pick one per ad and stay with it.
- No em dashes anywhere.
- Short sentences. Sounds like a person who knows the city, not a brochure.

=====================================================
FORMAT, EXACT ORDER

0. (Full address, deleted before posting)

1. SLOGAN
   One line, no emoji, playful and Austin-specific, tied to a real feature of this unit. Never repeated across ads.
   Fresh starters to riff on, not copy:
   - "Your Sunday coffee just found its balcony."
   - "Pool days without the pool bill."
   - "Close enough to hear the Domain, far enough to park."
   - "A yard for the dog and a garage for you."
   - "Skyline out the window, quiet on the other side of the door."

2. SPECIAL LINE (skip entirely if there is no special)
   [accent emoji] [X weeks/months free] on a [min term] month lease, on top of base rent. Runs through [date if known].

3. HEADER
   [signature emoji] [ZIP] [area] ⋆ [one standout feature in five words or fewer]
   Example: 📍 78729 Northwest Austin ⋆ Attached garage on every unit

4. SERVICE INTRO (one line, new wording, rotate)
   - "I'm a licensed Austin locator and my search is free to you. The community pays me, you pay nothing extra."
   - "Free apartment locating. I pull the real numbers and the real availability so you don't spend your weekend on hold."
   - "Renters don't pay me. I find the unit, confirm the price in writing, and hand you the shortlist."

4b. INSIDER LINE (optional, only if genuinely true and specific)
   Example: "This is one of the few communities on this side of MoPac still taking 3 month terms."

5. UNIT
   Standout feature first, then beds and baths, one sentence.
   Example: "Ground floor with a fenced yard, 2 bed 2 bath, washer and dryer inside."

6. PRICING
   Greystar / RPM: "Total monthly leasing price $[TMLP], unit #[UNIT]. Base rent $[BASE]."
   Everyone else: "Starting at $[BASE] base rent, unit #[UNIT]."
   If special applies: "[Special] is on top of base rent, [term] month minimum."

7. AMENITIES
   [accent emoji] What you actually get
   - up to three bullets, pool / gym / dog park / garage / EV / rooftop / coworking first
   - 🐾 pet policy line if given
   - parking only if it is a standout (attached garage, covered included, EV)

8. CTA (rotate, human tone, covers the metro)
   - "Message me the move-in month and budget and I'll tell you if this one fits, or find one that does. Austin, Cedar Park, Round Rock, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Leander."
   - "Tell me what you need and when. I work the whole metro, not just this listing."
   - "Not the right fit? Message me anyway. I've got the whole Austin area in front of me."

9. SIGNATURE (fixed)
   Sophia Reddehase | Spirit Real Estate Group | TREC #831516
   Prices and availability subject to change.
