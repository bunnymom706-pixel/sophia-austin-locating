Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with a licensed Texas apartment locator.

How is this service 100% free for renters? +
In Texas, apartment communities budget for marketing and pay licensed real estate locators a referral fee directly from their marketing accounts. Because this cost comes directly from the property management company, your rental rate, deposits, and move-in specials remain 100% identical (or better) than if you went alone.
How do I make sure you get credited as my locator? +
It is super simple: on every guest card, tour sign-in sheet, and online lease application, select "Real Estate Agent / Locator" under the referral question and write: Sophia Reddehase / Spirit Real Estate Group. If you already applied, simply notify your leasing agent or email them confirming Sophia as your locator.
Can you help me if I am moving from out of state? +
Yes! A large portion of my clients relocate from across the country. I provide personalized in-person video tours, FaceTime walkthroughs, floor plan measurements, and neighborhood vibe evaluations so you can sign with total peace of mind.
How quickly can I get a list of available apartments? +
Once you submit your criteria on my Spark VIP form, I typically deliver your custom interactive portal with live availability and unadvertised concessions within a few hours (maximum 24 hours). Urgent 48-hour move-ins receive priority rush handling.
Do you work with properties across all of Austin? +
Yes! I cover the entire Central Texas metropolitan region including Downtown, Rainey, East Austin, South Congress, South Lamar, The Domain, North Tech Ridge, Mueller, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Leander, Bee Cave, Westlake, and Lakeway.
