Questions I get every week

What does this cost me?

Nothing, ever. Apartment locating is free for renters in Texas. You’ll never get a bill, an invoice, or a surprise fee from me.

If it’s free, how do you get paid?

The apartment communities pay me a referral fee when you sign a lease — it comes out of their marketing budget, not your rent. All I ask is that you list my name as your referral source on the application so the property knows I sent you.

I have credit issues, an eviction, or a broken lease. Can you still help?

Yes — that’s my specialty. Tell me your situation up front and I’ll only send you communities that are more likely to work with it, so you’re not burning application fees on automatic denials.

How fast can you find me something?

I can usually have a shortlist to you within a day of getting your criteria. If you need to move this week, say so — I’ll focus on communities with immediate availability and fast approvals.

What areas do you cover?

The whole Austin metro — downtown and central Austin, the Domain and north Austin, east Austin, south Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Bee Cave, and the surrounding suburbs.

Do I have to tour apartments with you?

No. I can book you guided tours, self-guided tours, or send video walkthroughs — whatever fits your schedule. Plenty of my clients sign without me ever standing in the leasing office with them.

Are you actually a licensed agent?

Yes. I hold Texas real estate license #831516 and I’m sponsored by Spirit Real Estate Group, LLC. You can verify my license anytime on the TREC website.

What do you need from me to start?

Just the basics: area, budget, bedrooms, move date, and anything I should know about your credit or rental history. Text it to me or fill out my short form — it takes about a minute.
