The messages

First contact, within 5 minutes

Hey [Name]! Sophia here, licensed Austin locator. Free to you, the properties pay me. Four quick things so I don't waste your time: move-in date? Budget all-in? Areas you're leaning toward? Must-haves like pets or in-unit washer? And anything credit or lease-history wise I should know so I only send places that will approve you?

Follow-up schedule

  • Day 0: list sent same day. Five to eight places, each with the special, the commute, and one "why you" line.
  • Day 1: "Did any of those jump out? I can hold [Property]'s 10 weeks free through Friday."
  • Day 3: one new option or a changed special.
  • Day 7: "Still on for a [date] move? Specials rotate weekly, want a fresh list?"
  • Day 14: a 20-second video walkthrough of one unit.
  • Day 30: "If you already signed, congrats. If not, here's what changed."

The line that gets you paid

When you tour or apply, put "Sophia Reddehase / Spirit Real Estate Group" under "How did you hear about us" on the guest card and the application. That's how I get paid, and it costs you nothing.

Repeat this on every message. Most lost commissions are renters who toured on their own and forgot the guest card.

After move-in, and again at 60 days

So glad you're in! Two asks: a Google review helps me more than anything, and if a friend or coworker is moving, send them my way. When they sign I send you a $50 gift card, which is the legal max in Texas.

Getting the money in the door

  • Register your name before the tour. Many properties only pay if the locator was on the guest card at first contact. Confirm each property the night before.
  • Invoice within 48 hours of signing. Spirit invoices and collects for you, which is a real advantage. Your job is to get them the lease confirmation fast.
  • Tracker, one sheet: client, property, lease date, move-in, rent, fee %, invoice date, expected pay date, status. Anything past 60 days from move-in turns red.
  • Weekly 15-minute aging review. Day 31 for net-30 properties, day 61 for net-60, escalate to corporate at 90–120. This is the highest dollars-per-minute rep in the business.
  • Referral fees: $50 gift card max to unlicensed people. Never cash. Never more.

The money system that stops the loop

Scarcity research says the fix isn't willpower, it's converting every recurring decision into a one-time setup. Do this on the day your next commission lands, when your bandwidth is highest.

  1. One hub account. Every commission and every babysitting payment goes here first.
  2. On each deposit, same day: 25% to a tax account, 10% to a buffer account, rest stays in the hub.
  3. Pay yourself a fixed salary from the hub to a spending account on the 1st and 15th. Set it at your lowest realistic month, not your average. Good months build the hub, bad months draw on it, and your spending account never sees the swing.
  4. Autopay every fixed bill from one bills account. One account, one purpose, zero decisions.
  5. One card for all subscriptions. People guess $86 a month and actually pay $219. A single card makes the list visible and cancellation one freeze.
  6. Buffer first: $500, then $1,000, then one month. Families with $250–$750 in savings are 28% less likely to miss rent after an income shock. The first $500 does more than any later thousand.

Built for your brain

  • One inbox. Texts. Leads arrive there anyway. Instagram DMs get an auto-reply with your number. Email is checked in one scheduled block.
  • One list, one calendar. Todoist for tasks, Google Calendar for tours and the three reps as recurring events. Spark holds client state, not to-dos.
  • Templates at the point of action. Put all eight messages in your phone's text replacements so each is one tap. Use scheduled send for the day-3 and day-7 touches the moment you send day-1.
  • Body doubling for the tasks you avoid. Focusmate is free three times a week. Use it only for invoice chasing and cold-lead reengagement, the two things ADHD brains skip.
  • Theme days. Monday pipeline and invoices. Tuesday to Thursday tours in the afternoon, lists in the morning. Friday follow-ups and next week's tour booking. Weekend, one 20-minute inbox rep only.
  • Meds and sleep. Adderall XR at the same clock time daily so the peak window covers reps 1 and 2. Fixed wake time is the load-bearing variable. Finish hard exercise 3–4 hours before bed.
  • Impulsivity is the asset. The research on ADHD entrepreneurs says the "just message them, just drive there" energy predicts success. Inattention is the leak, and everything above plugs it with systems instead of willpower.
