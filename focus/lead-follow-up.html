Why the page is built this way

Holding your eyes on one point trains the same brain circuit that holds mental focus. Letting the gaze go wide afterward reduces "attentional blinks," the small lapses where you miss the next thing right in front of you. Writing down five names before the clock starts settles which brain network is in charge, so the task network runs the block instead of the wandering default network. The phone rule is the one habit the research ties most directly to attention loss.
