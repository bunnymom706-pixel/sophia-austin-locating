Why the page is built this way

One ad on screen at a time because working memory in ADHD holds about one thing. A progress line because finishing releases dopamine, and dopamine is what keeps the task network in charge. An eye drill at the start because the eyes and attention share a circuit. A wide-gaze reset at halfway because panoramic vision reduces attentional blinks. The phone rule because heavy phone use is the habit the research ties most directly to attention loss.

Source: Huberman Lab Essentials, ADHD & How Anyone Can Improve Their Focus.
