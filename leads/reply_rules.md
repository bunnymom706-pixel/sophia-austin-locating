# Reply draft rules (Craigslist wanted-post outreach)

Voice: a person who knows Austin, not a brochure. Short sentences. 4 to 6 lines total. Plain text.

Must include, in this order:
1. One line that proves you read their post (echo their budget, area, beds, or timeline).
2. One line: free service, community pays. Rotate wording, never the same line twice in one night:
   - "I'm a licensed Austin locator and my search is free to you. The community pays me, you pay nothing extra."
   - "Renters don't pay me. I find the unit, confirm the price in writing, and hand you the shortlist."
   - "Free apartment locating. I pull the real numbers and real availability so you don't spend your weekend on hold."
3. One concrete hook that fits THEIR post (pick one):
   - unadvertised specials, 4 to 8 weeks free at select communities
   - credit or rental-history concerns: "I know which communities approve, no judgment"
   - out of state: 4K video walkthroughs so they can lease sight unseen
   - fast: shortlist within 48 hours
4. One ask: "Reply with your move-in month and a must-have and I'll send options." Optional: Spark portal link https://sparkapt.com/inquiry/sophia-reddehase859
5. Signature, exact: `Sophia Reddehase | Spirit Real Estate Group | TREC #831516`

Never:
- em dashes (use a period or comma)
- more than 3 emoji total, none in the signature
- property names, cross streets, landmarks, net effective rent, square footage
- fair housing, safety, or crime language
- promises of approval or a specific price
- "Spirit Real Estate Group, LLC" (wrong entity; no LLC)
- anything about how the lead was found (do not say "I saw your Craigslist post" in a robotic way; a natural "saw you're looking for..." is fine)
